Syllabus Tracker workflow

  1. Complete Setup masters and staff mappings.
  2. Upload the correct Year Plan and verify class, programme and subject.
  3. Open Weekly Status; select week, class and subject.
  4. Enter current topic, periods taken, lagging periods and reason.
  5. Use Reports for teacher/class/subject/week-wise follow-up.

Year Plans should remain viewable and can be disabled when uploaded incorrectly instead of silently replacing records.
